# Literature

The documents the proofs follow are kept in this directory: the paper being formalized, everything it cites for a step, and the references for the textbook results the DAG splits.

A proof written by a human and checked by readers is worth more than one an agent writes, so get the document whenever a proof relies on one. A document you cannot obtain goes into the first table, and the launcher asks the human for it at once. When it arrives, the nodes waiting for it are rewritten from it, and its row moves to the second table.
